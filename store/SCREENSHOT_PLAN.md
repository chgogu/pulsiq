# PulsIQ — App Store screenshots

**Source device:** your iPhone 15 Pro Max (6.7", 2796 × 1290 px portrait).
These are accepted by App Store Connect for the required iPhone size.
(The simulator can't run PulsIQ — the ML Kit food-photo plugin has no arm64
simulator build — so capture on the real phone, which has your real data.)

## How to capture (cleanest = from your Mac)
1. Xcode → **Window → Devices and Simulators** → select your iPhone.
2. Open PulsIQ on the phone, navigate to the screen you want.
3. Click **Take Screenshot** in that Xcode window → a full-res PNG lands on
   your Desktop, already the exact size App Store needs.

Or on the phone: press **Side + Volume Up**, then AirDrop the shots to the Mac.

## Shot list (order matters — first 3 are what most users see)
Upload 5–6. Suggested captions (optional overlay text, or leave clean):

1. **Dashboard** — PulsIQ Score at top with "Today's log" right below it.
   Caption: "Your whole health picture — one score."
2. **Can I eat this?** — the big Yes/No verdict screen with the one-line reason.
   Caption: "Ask before you eat. A straight answer for your goal."
3. **Food log detail** — the fuel card showing calories + protein/carbs/fiber/
   fat/**sugar** rows.
   Caption: "Calories, macros, and sugar — logged in seconds."
4. **Body signals** — the separate Apple Health and WHOOP cards with a 30-day
   trend chart.
   Caption: "Apple Health and WHOOP, side by side."
5. **Voice log** — the pulse button mid-recording (or the parsed result).
   Caption: "Just talk. PulsIQ logs it."
6. **Privacy** — the security/app-lock or data-export screen.
   Caption: "Encrypted on your device. Never sold."

## Upload
App Store Connect → your app → iOS 1.0 → **Previews and Screenshots** →
drag the PNGs into the **6.7"** iPhone slot. That one set covers all iPhone
sizes; you don't need to shoot others.
