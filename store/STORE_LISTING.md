## Full description  (max 4000)
```
PulsIQ brings your whole health picture into one place — your heart, your
sleep, your recovery, and everything you eat — and turns it into plain-language
guidance you can act on today. Every number is shown against your own baseline,
never as raw jargon.

CAN I EAT THIS?
Not sure about a food? Type it, scan the barcode, or snap a photo and get a
single, honest answer — yes or no — with a one-line reason tuned to your goal,
whether that's weight loss, weight gain, diabetes, pregnancy, or heart health.
No hype, no guilt, no biased nudging.

LOG FOOD IN SECONDS
• Hold the pulse button and just talk — "grande oat latte and a burrito bowl."
  PulsIQ parses it on your device and logs it instantly.
• Scan a barcode or snap a plate to capture calories and macros automatically.
• See protein, carbs, fiber, fat — and sugar — for everything you eat, so you
  can actually keep daily sugar in check.

YOUR BODY SIGNALS, SIDE BY SIDE
• Connect Apple Health and WHOOP independently — each gets its own card, so
  turning one off never hides the other.
• Watch the PulsIQ Score (0–100) blend recovery, sleep, fuel quality, and
  hydration into one number, with a live breakdown.
• See 30-day trend charts for heart rate, HRV, sleep, and recovery — only the
  metrics your devices actually track, nothing faked.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Health data is encrypted on your device. Optional cloud backup stores only
  ciphertext — the key never leaves your phone.
• No passwords (Sign in with Google or a passkey), a biometric app lock, and an
  in-app audit trail of every health-data access.
• No third-party analytics. Your data is never sold or shared.
• Export everything as JSON or delete it all, any time, in a couple of taps.

PulsIQ PLUS
Unlock cloud-powered food estimates and richer coaching for $1.99/month. The
core app — logging, tracking, and your body-signal cards — is always free.

PulsIQ is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Nothing in the app is
medical advice — for any health concern, talk to a clinician.
```
